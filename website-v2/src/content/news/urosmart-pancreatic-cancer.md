---
title: "UroSmart: Non-Invasive Aptasensor for Early Pancreatic Cancer Detection Published"
date: 2026-09-01
summary: "Our UroSmart platform for point-of-care detection of the pancreatic cancer biomarker CA 19.9 in urine has been published in Biosensors and Bioelectronics: X."
---
Our fully integrated UroSmart platform — combining aseptic urine sample collection, automated microfluidic dispensing, and aptamer-based electrochemical sensing — detects CA 19.9, the only clinically approved biomarker for pancreatic cancer, at the point of care. The system achieves a limit of detection of 0.47 U/mL in buffer and 2.02 U/mL in human urine, establishing a strong foundation for accessible, urine-based cancer screening.
