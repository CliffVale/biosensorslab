We reported an aptamer-based SARS-CoV-2 salivary antigen assay employing only low-cost reagents ($3.20/test) and an off-the-shelf glucometer. The test was engineered around a glucometer because it is quantitative, easy to use, and the most prevalent piece of diagnostic equipment globally — making the test highly scalable with infrastructure that is already in place.
