An aptamer-based field effect transistor (aptaFET) biosensor, developed using an extended gate field effect transistor with inter-digitated gold microelectrodes (IDµE), for the detection of the malaria biomarker *Plasmodium falciparum* glutamate dehydrogenase (PfGDH) in serum samples. A 90-mer long ssDNA aptamer (NG3) selective to PfGDH is used in the aptaFET to capture the target protein.
