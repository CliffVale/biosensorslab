UroSmart combines aseptic urine sample collection, automated microfluidic dispensing, and aptamer-based electrochemical sensing in a single user-friendly device for point-of-care detection of CA 19.9, the only clinically approved biomarker for pancreatic cancer. The system achieves a limit of detection of 0.47 U/mL in buffer and 2.02 U/mL in human urine across a broad linear range of 0.1–1000 U/mL, with high specificity and reproducibility — establishing a strong foundation for accessible, urine-based cancer screening beyond traditional laboratory settings.
