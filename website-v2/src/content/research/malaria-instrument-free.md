We reported the detection of pan malaria and *P. falciparum* species using a dye-based reaction catalyzed by the biomarker enzymes Plasmodium lactate dehydrogenase (pLDH) and Plasmodium falciparum glutamate dehydrogenase (PfGDH), respectively, through instrument-based and instrument-free approaches. The colorimetric and smartphone-read assays are designed for resource-limited settings, making malaria screening accessible without laboratory infrastructure.
