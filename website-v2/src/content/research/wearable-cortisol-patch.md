We reported a wearable patch based on soft microfluidics and flexible electrodes functionalized with a pseudoknot-assisted conformation-switching aptamer for pH-calibrated, continuous, non-invasive cortisol monitoring in sweat. This wearable electrochemical sensor exhibited a broad dynamic range (1 pM – 10 µM) with a sub-picomolar limit of detection in sweat. The patch was validated by measuring cortisol in real time on three human subjects with induced stress.
