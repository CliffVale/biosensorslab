Aptamers are "chemical antibodies" with great potential for applications where antibodies are used. However, traditional methods for aptamer selection are time-consuming and labor-intensive. Our lab has developed a totally new concept for aptamer discovery — **Hydrogel for Aptamer Selection (HAS)**. HAS allows us to select aptamers in a single step, taking only 60 hours. The concept was published in *Nature Biotechnology* (2023).
