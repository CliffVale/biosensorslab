Dr. Manisha is currently working as Project Research Scientist-I at BiosensorsLab, Centre for Biomedical Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology, Delhi. She has completed her Ph.D. in Chemical Sciences from Advanced Materials and Device Metrology Division, CSIR-National Physical Laboratory, New Delhi.

Her ongoing research work focuses on the development of point-of-care diagnostic technologies, electrochemical biosensors and conducting polymers in biomedical applications. She is an active member of Metrology Society of India and has also been a recipient of the ANRF-International Travel Support (ITS) for international academic exposure and participation in FAPS 2025, organized by Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore.
