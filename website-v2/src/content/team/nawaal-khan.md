Nawaal Khan is pursuing her Ph.D. at the Centre for Biomedical Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Delhi. She holds a Master's degree in Biotechnology from Jamia Millia Islamia (JMI), New Delhi.

Her PhD research focuses on multifunctional biosensing and microneedle-based drug delivery systems for ocular disease management, currently targeting glaucoma. She has gained research experience through internships and collaborative projects at the Special Centre of Molecular Medicine, Jawaharlal Nehru University (JNU), New Delhi. Her academic achievements have been recognized with multiple merit scholarships and prestigious national-level competitive examinations, including CSIR-NET and the ICMR Junior Research Fellowship.
